Macro Tracker Spec
Users
Two people (me and my fiancée), each with our own login and private log.

Core screens
Today: meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks) with daily totals vs. targets
Add food: search, pick, enter portion, choose meal, save
History: any past day, weekly averages
Settings: my calorie and macro targets
Data sources
USDA FoodData Central for whole foods
Open Food Facts for barcodes (Phase 2)
Out of scope for now
Recipes, social features, exercise tracking, public app store release

Rules
Installable PWA, works on iPhone Safari and Android Chrome
Mobile-first layout
Macro math is done in code, never estimated by AI
